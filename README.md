A two-line robot confession for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59): one sentence owning a specific mistake, one naming the lesson and the fix.

> I am sorry I gave the cat the dog's medicine because the bottles looked the same.
> I have learned that identical bottles are not identical doses, and I will now read the name on the label before I open anything.

`main.ts` writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (210 bytes, no trailing newline).
